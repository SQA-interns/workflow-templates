# US-006 Add a Contact

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Editor, I want to add a Contact with its Fields to the current Group, so that the whole Group can find that person or company.

## Purpose

Contacts are the core of the product; the brief predefines name, surname, address, country, e-mail and company (Q-10). A company without a person must be possible (Q-20).

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Contact form | The form with all Fields of `business-rules.md` (Data model, Contact) in that order. |
| Name part | firstName, lastName or company. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-006-01 | When the Owner or an Editor saves the Contact form, the system shall create the Contact in the current Group with Version 1 of kind `created`. | Q-09, Q-20, BR-05 |
| R-006-02 | If every name part is blank after trimming, then the system shall reject the Contact with "Enter a first name, last name or company." and store nothing. | Q-20 |
| R-006-03 | If a Field is longer than its maximum length (Data model), then the system shall reject the Contact with "<Field> may have at most <n> characters." and store nothing. | Q-10 |
| R-006-04 | If the e-mail is not blank and is not a syntactically valid e-mail address, then the system shall reject the Contact with "Enter a valid e-mail address." and store nothing. | Q-10 |
| R-006-05 | If the country is not blank and is not an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code, then the system shall reject the Contact with HTTP 400 and store nothing; the UI offers only a list of countries. | Q-10 |
| R-006-06 | When the e-mail entered equals the e-mail of an active Contact of the same Group, the system shall show "A Contact with this e-mail already exists: <name>." and still allow saving. | Q-21 |
| R-006-07 | The system shall trim leading and trailing spaces of every text Field before saving. [ASSUMPTION] not given. | — |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-006-01 | Marko is Editor of "Konferenca 2025" | he saves first name "Janez", last name "Novak", e-mail `janez.novak@example.si`, phone "+386 41 123 456", company "Podjetje d.o.o.", job title "Direktor", street "Slovenska cesta 1", postal code "1000", city "Ljubljana", country SI, note "Govornik" | "Janez Novak" is in the Contact list and its History shows Version 1 `created` by Marko Kranjc | R-006-01 |
| AC-006-02 | as AC-006-01 | he saves only company "Podjetje d.o.o." | the Contact is created | R-006-01, R-006-02 |
| AC-006-03 | as AC-006-01 | he saves only e-mail `info@example.si` | "Enter a first name, last name or company." is shown and no Contact is stored | R-006-02 |
| AC-006-04 | as AC-006-01 | he saves first name "Janez" and e-mail `janez@` | "Enter a valid e-mail address." and nothing is stored | R-006-04 |
| AC-006-05 | "Janez Novak" with `janez.novak@example.si` exists | Marko enters `Janez.Novak@example.si` for a new Contact "J. Novak" | he sees "A Contact with this e-mail already exists: Janez Novak." and saving creates the second Contact | R-006-06, BR-11 |
| AC-006-06 | Petra is Viewer | she posts a new Contact to the API | HTTP 403 and nothing is stored | BR-03 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-006-01 | firstName of 100 characters | Saved | R-006-03 |
| EX-006-02 | firstName of 101 characters | "First name may have at most 100 characters."; nothing stored | R-006-03 |
| EX-006-03 | note of 2000 / 2001 characters | Saved / rejected | R-006-03 |
| EX-006-04 | email of 254 / 255 characters (valid syntax) | Saved / rejected | R-006-03 |
| EX-006-05 | firstName "   ", lastName "", company "  " | "Enter a first name, last name or company." | R-006-02 |
| EX-006-06 | country "XX" via the API | HTTP 400; nothing stored | R-006-05 |
| EX-006-07 | firstName "  Janez " | Stored as "Janez" | R-006-07 |
| EX-006-08 | Name with diacritics "Žiga Čuk" | Stored and shown as "Žiga Čuk" | R-006-01 |
| EX-006-09 | Contact posted with the `groupId` of a Group Marko is not a Member of | HTTP 404; nothing stored | BR-02 |

## Out of scope

Attributes (US-008) and Tags (US-009) on the Contact form; import; photos; several e-mails or phones per Contact; validating that a phone number exists.
