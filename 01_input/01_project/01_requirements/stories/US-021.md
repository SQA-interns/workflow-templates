# US-021 Delete my account

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As a User, I want to delete my account, so that the product no longer keeps my personal data.

## Purpose

A User's account data is kept until the User deletes the account (Q-33, OPEN — needs customer).

## Definitions

None.

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-021-01 | When a User confirms "Delete my account" by typing their e-mail address, the system shall delete the User's Memberships, the User and the Firebase account. | Q-33 (OPEN — needs customer) |
| R-021-02 | If the User is the Owner of a Group that is active or in the recycle bin, then the system shall reject the deletion with "Delete your Groups first: <names>." and change nothing. [ASSUMPTION] handing over ownership is out of scope (US-005). | Q-33 |
| R-021-03 | When a User is deleted, the system shall show "Deleted user" instead of their display name in Versions. [ASSUMPTION] not given; removes the personal data. | Q-32, Q-33 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-021-01 | Marko is Editor of "Konferenca 2025" and made Version 2 of "Janez Novak" | he confirms "Delete my account" with `marko.kranjc@gmail.com` | he is signed out and cannot sign in; Ana's Members screen no longer lists him; Version 2 shows "Deleted user" | R-021-01, R-021-03 |
| AC-021-02 | Ana owns "Konferenca 2025" | she chooses "Delete my account" | "Delete your Groups first: Konferenca 2025." and nothing changes | R-021-02 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-021-01 | Typed e-mail does not match | "Delete" stays disabled; nothing changes | R-021-01 |
| EX-021-02 | Owner of a Group in the recycle bin only | Rejected, the Group is named | R-021-02 |
| EX-021-03 | A request with the deleted User's old ID token | HTTP 401 | R-021-01, R-001-03 |

## Out of scope

Exporting one's own data; a waiting period before the deletion.
