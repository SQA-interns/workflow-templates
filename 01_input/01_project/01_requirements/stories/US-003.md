# US-003 Rename, delete and restore a Group

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner, I want to rename my Group, move it to the recycle bin and restore it within the recycle bin period, so that I can keep the Group's name right and remove a finished project without losing it by mistake.

## Purpose

Only the Owner may rename or delete the Group (Q-18). Deleting a Group goes through the recycle bin, after which all its data is removed (Q-33).

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Deleted Groups | The Owner's list of their Groups that are in the recycle bin. |
| Removal date | `deletedAt` + recycle bin period (`business-rules.md`, project-wide facts), shown in Europe/Ljubljana. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-003-01 | When the Owner renames the Group, the system shall save the new name, validated as in R-002-02. | Q-18 (OPEN — needs customer) |
| R-003-02 | When the Owner confirms "Move Group to recycle bin", the system shall set the Group to `inRecycleBin` with `deletedAt` = now. | Q-33 (OPEN — needs customer) |
| R-003-03 | While a Group is `inRecycleBin`, the system shall treat every request to its data, from any User, as for a Group the User is not a Member of (BR-02), except the Owner's "Deleted Groups" list and restore. | Q-33 |
| R-003-04 | While a Group is `inRecycleBin`, the system shall list it in the Owner's "Deleted Groups" with its name and removal date. | Q-33 |
| R-003-05 | When the Owner restores a Group before its removal date, the system shall set it to `active` with all its Contacts, Members, Invitations and Tags as they were. | Q-33 |
| R-003-06 | If the Owner tries to restore a Group at or after its removal date, then the system shall reject it with "This Group can no longer be restored." and change nothing. | Q-33 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-003-01 | Ana owns "Konferenca 2025" | she renames it to "Konferenca 2026" | the Group switcher shows "Konferenca 2026" | R-003-01 |
| AC-003-02 | Ana owns "Konferenca 2025"; Marko is its Editor | Marko renames it to "Test" | the response is HTTP 403 "not allowed" and the name stays "Konferenca 2025" | BR-04 |
| AC-003-03 | Ana owns "Konferenca 2025" with Marko as Editor | Ana confirms "Move Group to recycle bin" on 05.03.2026 at 11:00 Europe/Ljubljana | the Group is gone from both switchers; "Deleted Groups" shows "Konferenca 2025" with removal date "04.04.2026 12:00" (DST started in between); Marko's `GET /api/v1/groups/<id>` returns HTTP 404; an Audit entry is written | R-003-02, R-003-03, R-003-04, BR-10 |
| AC-003-04 | "Konferenca 2025" with 3 Contacts is in the recycle bin since 05.03.2026 11:00 | Ana restores it on 10.03.2026 | it is back in both switchers with its 3 Contacts and Marko as Editor; an Audit entry is written | R-003-05, BR-10 |
| AC-003-05 | "Konferenca 2025" was moved to the recycle bin 30 days ago | the removal date passes | the Group, its Contacts, Versions, Members, Invitations and Tags no longer exist; "Deleted Groups" no longer shows it | BR-08 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-003-01 | Restore one minute before the removal date | Restored | R-003-05 |
| EX-003-02 | Restore exactly at the removal date, before the removal job ran | Rejected "This Group can no longer be restored." | R-003-06 |
| EX-003-03 | A Viewer moves the Group to the recycle bin | HTTP 403, nothing changes | BR-04 |
| EX-003-04 | Owner cancels the confirmation | Nothing changes | R-003-02, BR-07 |
| EX-003-05 | An Editor opens an Invitation link of a Group in the recycle bin | "This Invitation is no longer valid." | R-003-03 |
| EX-003-06 | Rename with a 101-character name | Rejected, name unchanged | R-003-01 |

## Out of scope

Handing the Group over to another Owner; deleting a Group immediately without the recycle bin; exporting a whole Group before deletion (Export is US-016).
