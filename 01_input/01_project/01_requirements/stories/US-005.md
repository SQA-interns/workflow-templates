# US-005 Change a Member's Role or remove a Member

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As an Owner, I want to see the Members of my Group, change a Member between Editor and Viewer, and remove a Member, so that only the right people can see and change our Contacts.

## Purpose

People join and leave a project; the Owner manages the Members (Q-18).

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Members screen | The Owner's list of Members with display name, e-mail and Role. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-005-01 | The system shall show the Members screen, and return the Member list from the API, only to the Owner. [ASSUMPTION] who else may see Members is not stated; Members' e-mails are personal data. | Q-18 (OPEN — needs customer) |
| R-005-02 | When the Owner changes a Member's Role between Editor and Viewer, the system shall apply the new Role to every later request of that Member. | Q-18 |
| R-005-03 | When the Owner confirms "Remove from Group" for a Member, the system shall delete the Membership. | Q-18 |
| R-005-04 | The system shall keep the Versions made by a removed Member and show them with that Member's display name. | Q-23 |
| R-005-05 | If the Owner tries to change their own Role or remove themselves, then the system shall reject it with "A Group always has one Owner." and change nothing. [ASSUMPTION] handing over ownership is not stated. | — |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-005-01 | "Konferenca 2025": Ana Owner, Marko Editor, Petra Viewer | Ana opens the Members screen | it lists Ana Kovač – Owner, Marko Kranjc – Editor, Petra Horvat – Viewer with their e-mails | R-005-01 |
| AC-005-02 | as AC-005-01 | Ana changes Marko to Viewer, then Marko edits a Contact | the edit is rejected with HTTP 403; an Audit entry for the Role change is written | R-005-02, BR-03, BR-10 |
| AC-005-03 | as AC-005-01; Marko made Version 2 of "Janez Novak" | Ana removes Marko | "Konferenca 2025" is not in Marko's Group switcher; his `GET /api/v1/groups/<id>/contacts` returns HTTP 404; the History of "Janez Novak" still shows Version 2 by "Marko Kranjc"; an Audit entry is written | R-005-03, R-005-04, BR-02, BR-10 |
| AC-005-04 | as AC-005-01 | Petra calls `GET /api/v1/groups/<id>/members` | the response is HTTP 403 | R-005-01 |
| AC-005-05 | as AC-005-01 | Marko changes Petra to Editor through the API | HTTP 403; Petra stays Viewer | BR-04 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-005-01 | Ana removes herself | "A Group always has one Owner."; nothing changes | R-005-05 |
| EX-005-02 | Ana sets her own Role to Editor | "A Group always has one Owner."; nothing changes | R-005-05 |
| EX-005-03 | Role change to Owner through the API | HTTP 400; nothing changes | R-005-02 |
| EX-005-04 | Ana cancels the removal confirmation | Nothing changes | R-005-03 |
| EX-005-05 | A removed Member is invited again and accepts | Member again with the new Role | R-005-03, R-004-04 |

## Out of scope

Handing over ownership; a Member leaving a Group on their own; showing the Member list to Editors and Viewers.
