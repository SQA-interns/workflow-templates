# US-017 Find Possible duplicates

> For: agent (reads), human (reviews) · Owner: Product owner · Agent: read-only

Format: `general/requirements-standard.md`.

## Story

As a Member, I want to see the pairs of Contacts that may be the same person or company, with the reason, so that I can clean up the Group's Contacts.

## Purpose

The customer expects advanced features such as finding duplicate contacts (Q-09 F6); finding them is a success criterion (Q-05).

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Normalised phone | The phone with every character removed except digits and a leading `+`. |
| Normalised full name | firstName and lastName joined by one space, as Normalised text (US-014), with repeated spaces reduced to one. |
| Name similarity | Trigram similarity of two Normalised full names, from 0 to 1. |

## Rules

| ID | Rule (EARS) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R-017-01 | When a Member opens "Possible duplicates", the system shall list every pair of active Contacts of the current Group that meets at least one of R-017-02 to R-017-04, with every reason it meets. | Q-25 |
| R-017-02 | The system shall treat two Contacts as a Possible duplicate when both e-mails are non-blank and equal after trimming, case-insensitively (reason "Same e-mail"). | Q-25, BR-11 |
| R-017-03 | The system shall treat two Contacts as a Possible duplicate when both Normalised phones have at least 6 digits and are equal (reason "Same phone"). [ASSUMPTION] minimum length not given. | Q-25 |
| R-017-04 | The system shall treat two Contacts as a Possible duplicate when their Name similarity is at least 0.8 and both companies are non-blank and equal case-insensitively (reason "Similar name, same company"). [ASSUMPTION] threshold 0.8 not given. | Q-25 |
| R-017-05 | The system shall only suggest Possible duplicates and never change a Contact because of them. | Q-25, BR-07 |

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given | When | Then | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-017-01 | "Janez Novak" `janez.novak@example.si` and "J. Novak" `Janez.Novak@example.si` | Marko opens "Possible duplicates" | the pair is listed with "Same e-mail" | R-017-01, R-017-02 |
| AC-017-02 | "Janez Novak" phone "+386 41 123 456", "Janez N." phone "+386-41-123-456" | Marko opens "Possible duplicates" | the pair is listed with "Same phone" | R-017-03 |
| AC-017-03 | "Janez Novak" and "Janez Novack", both company "Podjetje d.o.o." | Marko opens "Possible duplicates" | the pair is listed with "Similar name, same company" | R-017-04 |
| AC-017-04 | "Janez Novak" (Podjetje d.o.o.) and "Janez Novak" (Alfa d.o.o.), no shared e-mail or phone | Marko opens "Possible duplicates" | the pair is not listed | R-017-04 |
| AC-017-05 | any Possible duplicates | Marko opens and closes the list | no Contact has a new Version | R-017-05 |

## Edge cases

| ID | Input or situation | Expected result | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| EX-017-01 | Both e-mails blank | Not a pair by e-mail | R-017-02 |
| EX-017-02 | Phones "12345" and "12345" (5 digits) | Not a pair by phone | R-017-03 |
| EX-017-03 | Same e-mail and same phone | One pair with both reasons | R-017-01 |
| EX-017-04 | One of the pair is in the recycle bin or merged | Not listed | BR-09 |
| EX-017-05 | Same e-mail in two different Groups | Not listed | BR-01 |
| EX-017-06 | Names "Žiga Čuk" and "Ziga Cuk", same company | Listed (similarity 1.0) | R-017-04 |
| EX-017-07 | No pairs | "No possible duplicates found." | R-017-01 |

## Out of scope

Dismissing a pair as "not a duplicate"; duplicates across Groups; automatic merging; matching "041 123 456" with "+386 41 123 456".
