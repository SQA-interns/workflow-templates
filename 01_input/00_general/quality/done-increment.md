# Definition of Done: increment (`DoD-I`)

> For: agent · Owner: QA lead · Agent: read-only

Checked by `/review` for every increment in `docs/02_tasks.md`; evidence in `docs/04_review-<INC>.md`.

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| DoD-I-01 | Every story of the increment meets `DoD-S`. |
| DoD-I-02 | The review finds no Critical or High finding left open. |
| DoD-I-03 | The application starts and the increment's flows work at runtime, not only in tests. |
| DoD-I-04 | Screenshots of every screen of the increment at the three widths (UI-22) match the approved wireframes. |
